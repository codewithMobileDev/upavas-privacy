Upavas privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Upavas is an intermittent fasting and weight tracker made by Anandhu Murali. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

The short version

All your data stays on your device. Upavas does not collect it, and the developer never sees it. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not connect to the internet.

What you enter, and where it is kept

Upavas stores the following in a private database on your device:

  • your profile: name (optional), sex for calculations, birth year, height, starting weight, and a target weight to reach or hold
  • your weigh-ins, fasts, walks and workouts, including any notes you add
  • how you felt during and after a fast (hunger, energy and mood), if you choose to log it
  • water glasses, and body measurements such as waist, hips, chest, arm and thigh
  • your settings, such as units, language, reminder times, fasting plan and planned fasting days

The app uses these only to show your progress, estimates, insights and reports, and to schedule reminders on your device.

Progress photos

If you add progress photos, Upavas keeps a copy in its own private folder on your device. Other apps cannot read that folder. When you pick a photo from your gallery, Android's photo picker gives Upavas only the photo you choose, not your whole gallery. Photos are not included in backup files. Deleting a photo in the app, using Reset all data, or uninstalling the app removes them.

Steps

If you turn on step counting, Upavas reads today's step count from your phone's motion sensor and shows it on screen. Steps are not stored and never leave your device. Step counting is off until you turn it on.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) checks it is you. Upavas only learns whether the check passed. It never receives or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

Home-screen widget

If you add the Upavas widget, it shows your current fasting plan and end time on your home screen, where anyone who can see your screen can read it. Remove the widget to hide this.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to send it. These features let you do that:

  • Share a monthly report as a PDF
  • Export a backup as a JSON file
  • Export your weigh-ins, fasts and activities as CSV files

Each one opens your device's share sheet, and you pick where the file goes. Once you send a file to another app or person, that app or person's own privacy terms apply. These files contain your health information, so please share them with care.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Upavas turns off Android's automatic backup to Google Drive, so your data is not copied to the cloud. If you use Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer, the system may copy app data between your own devices. To keep your data when you change phones, you can also export a backup and import it on the new phone. Progress photos are not in the backup file.

Uninstalling the app or using Reset all data deletes your data from the device. Files you have already exported are not affected, so delete those separately.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your device, with no server involved. Their text may appear on your lock screen, depending on your device's settings. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device's settings.

Permissions

Upavas asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them. You can refuse any of them, and the rest of the app still works.

  • Notifications: for reminders.
  • Camera: to take progress photos inside the app.
  • Physical activity: to count steps, if you turn step counting on.

It also uses permissions that need no prompt: your phone's fingerprint or face unlock for the optional app lock, restoring reminders after a restart, vibration, keeping the device awake briefly to deliver a reminder, and letting Android refresh the home-screen widget in the background. It does not have permission to use the internet.

Google Play

If you install Upavas from Google Play, Google may collect information about your installation and device under its own privacy policy. The developer may see the anonymous, summarised statistics that Google Play Console provides. Upavas itself sends nothing.

Age

Upavas is for adults aged 18 and over, and it is not directed at children.

Health information

Upavas gives estimates, not medical advice. Please talk to your doctor before changing your diet.
